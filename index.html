About AAFWS

Advocate & Advocate Family Welfare Society

Our Mission

The Advocate & Advocate Family Welfare Society (AAFWS) is a dedicated platform committed to the financial, professional, medical, and social welfare of advocates across India and their families. We believe that those who uphold justice deserve equal protection, dignity, and institutional support.

Vision

A nation where every advocate — regardless of seniority or financial status — practices with dignity, security, and professional pride.

Core Values

Justice · Dignity · Solidarity · Empowerment · Transparency · Inclusive Welfare

"Those who spend their lives protecting justice and constitutional rights deserve dignity, financial security, healthcare protection, professional infrastructure, and welfare support for themselves as well."

— AAFWS FOUNDING CHARTER
